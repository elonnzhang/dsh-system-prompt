- `deepseek-harness/apps/cli/config/agent-presets/minimal/agent.cordis.yml:1-14` sets `complete: true`, `includeRuntimeContext: false`, and the fixed persona.
- `deepseek-harness/apps/cli/tests/web-agent-presets.e2e.ts:249-263` expects one `deployment:persona` section and only `bash` plus `str_replace_editor`.

However, the historical target payload carried `agentPreset: minimal` while exposing 21 sections and 35 tools. The isolated `52721` process reproduced both shapes for two different live sessions: one `minimal` session with `1` section and `5` tools, and another with `21` sections and `35` tools. Therefore the durable header value `minimal` is not sufficient evidence of the effective composition for this historical session. The exact composition at the time of `session-8e05...` cannot be reconstructed from the empty Markdown file or from a fresh isolated process.
